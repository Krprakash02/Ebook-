# Part I — Passwords and Account Security

Every account you own starts with the same question: *how does this service know it's really you?* For most of us, the answer is a password, and for most of us, that password is doing more work, and carrying more risk, than we realise.

This part covers five habits: reusing passwords, choosing guessable ones, ignoring breach news, skipping multi-factor authentication, and storing or sharing passwords carelessly. None needs technical skill to fix. Together, they make the biggest difference to how easy or hard you are to break into.

**A quick promise:** you won't be asked to memorise twenty complicated passwords. You will be shown how to need only one or two.
